## What's new

- **Stages for writing.** A project can have a list of stages (detail panel of the project → *More fields* → *Stages*): *Long text* (Outline → First draft → Revision → Beta readers → Polished), *Article* (Idea → Draft → Edit → Published), or your own list. Every entry below that project then has a **Stage** menu, and its stage shows as a coloured chip in the tree, on *Today* and on the *Project* page. The Project page counts the entries at each stage.
- **The last stage means done.** An entry with a stage is not finished when its word count is full: a chapter with all its words is at the end of its stage, and the app offers the next one. Setting the last stage sets the entry to done; going back from it opens it again. Entries without a stage work as before.
- **Ongoing: places that are never finished.** Tick *Ongoing* in the detail panel of a place such as Inbox, Personal or Writing. The app then does not offer to mark it done when all its sub-items are done.
- **Two new growing pictures.** **Tree**: a seed, a sprout, then leaf after leaf, blossoms from the half and fruit from three quarters. **Night sky**: star after star comes out and joins to a constellation, and the moon fills up. What you did today is in the entry's colour, as in the other pictures.
- **Finished pictures sparkle.** The stars of a picture at 100 % twinkle. (They stand still when your system is set to show fewer animations.)
- **Drag and drop shows where the entry goes.** The place is a framed slot that starts where the entry's content starts, at its real indent. The entry it will become a sub-item of is marked with a bar on its left. The row you drag hangs beside the pointer, so it does not cover the slot.
- **The Inbox is a project with its own icon.** An Inbox the app makes (at the first start, after *Start with empty data*, or with the first Quick add) is an ongoing project with the inbox icon. An Inbox you already have does not change: set its type to *Project*, pick the icon and tick *Ongoing* if you want the same.
- **URL and Discord webhook sit low in the detail panel**, above *Links & attachments*: they are set once and then left alone. An entry without a URL has the field behind *More fields*.
- **Demo data:** the top entries are ongoing, and the manuscript shows the stages, with chapters from *Outline* to *Polished*.

## Fixes

- **Move to…** (right-click menu) no longer opens the entry the others were moved into. It stays open or closed, as it was.

## For people who build the app

- New fields: `ongoing` (true, or absent), `stages` (on a project: up to 12 names) and `stage` (on an entry: one name). The API, the command line (`todo set ID ongoing=true`) and MCP take them. The rule "the last stage means done" is in the page; the server stores the fields.
- `pic` also takes `tree` and `sky`.
- The Inbox the server makes (`todo add`, MCP, CSV / calendar import) has `kind: project`, `status: active`, `ongoing: true` and `icon: inbox`. Because a project is not counted as an open todo, a new Inbox no longer adds 1 to the "open" number of the statistics.
