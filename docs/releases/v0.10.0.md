## What's new

- **Writing goals on Today.** Each open word or page goal shows at the top of *Today*: today's amount, a bar for what you wrote today, the words left and when you will be done. A missed day spreads the rest over the remaining days. A repeating goal such as "Write 500 words" leaves Today once today's words are written. A chapter that waits for the one before it (or a postponed goal) only shows up when it can start.
- **Growing pictures.** Give a manuscript or another goal a picture (detail panel → Progress → *Picture*): a stack of pages that becomes a bound book with your title, or a walk across a map to a castle. It grows with every word, from 0 to 100 %, and shows on *Today* and in the detail panel. A project counts the words of its chapters. The demo data shows both.
- **Write with the timer.** The clock next to a writing goal on *Today* starts the timer for it. When it stops, the time is logged and the app asks how many words you wrote (`+600`, or the new total).
- **Log words in two clicks.** *+ Log* on Today, or a click on the "words left" tag in the tree: type `+500` and press Enter. Scripts can add words with `{"prog":{"add":500}}`.
- **Word and LibreOffice files in the notes viewer.** Attached `.docx` and `.odt` files now show their text.
- **Drop a calendar file on Quick add.** Drop one or more `.ics` files (or CSV files) onto the *Quick add* field to import them; several files share one preview, and each gets its own project. A preview lists the entries first: untick the ones you do not want, then click *Import*. Entries that are already there are greyed out. Past events are listed too, but not ticked (before, they were always left out). Untick *Put them into a new project* to add the entries to the Inbox instead. *Import CSV / calendar…* in the ⋯ menu shows the same preview.
- **The detail panel header stays in view.** The row with *Todo / Idea / Project / Reminder*, the id and the close button stays at the top while you scroll the panel.
- **AI on this computer (optional, off and NOT installed by default).** A small AI that runs on your own computer, without an account and without the internet. *Suggest sub-tasks* splits an entry into steps, or adds the missing ones. *Rewrite* above the notes can **tidy up** the notes (spelling, lists, tick boxes, groups) or **expand** them with next steps, things to prepare, open questions and tips. You always see the suggestion first and choose. Set it up once: put `llama-server` and a model file into a folder `ai` next to the program, then Settings → AI → *Use AI*. The README explains which files to download, and has a section on safety and one on memory and speed. The AI starts at the first click and stops after 15 minutes without use, or at once with *Unload*. With several model files in `ai/`, Settings → AI lets you pick one. Bigger models give much better answers but need more memory; the README has a rough guide.
- **Suggest estimate.** Next to *Estimate* in the detail panel, or in the right-click menu: a suggestion from your own finished todos with tracked time, without AI. A repeating todo uses its own past times. The reason names the todos it used, and you click *Use* to take it. With no such history and AI on, the AI makes a guess.
- **Summarize an attachment (AI).** The robot button next to an attached mail, text, Word or LibreOffice file (up to 20,000 characters, about 8 pages; `aiMaxChars` in `data/config.json` changes it) gives a summary, key points, dates and to-dos, to add to the notes or as sub-tasks. Commands hidden in a file are ignored, links the AI adds are removed, and a red warning shows when a file tries to trick the AI or looks like phishing.
- **Quick add in normal words.** `Dentist tomorrow at 3pm`, `remind me on Friday to call Anna` or `Zahnarzt morgen um 15 Uhr` get their date, time and type without AI. A message shows how the text was read, with *Keep as typed*; Shift+Enter always adds as typed. Noon / Mittag and midnight / Mitternacht work too. With AI on, harder texts (`at the end of the month`, a dropped mail paragraph) go to the AI, with a preview first; Ctrl+Enter always asks the AI. Dropped text is always kept whole and unchanged as the notes.
- **Suggest tags.** *Suggest* next to *Tags* shows your existing tags that appear in the name or notes; click to add. Only tags you already have, so no near-duplicates. With no match and AI on, the AI picks from your tags.

## Fixes

- An event or reminder of today whose time is over no longer shows under *Upcoming reminders* on Today. It moves to a dimmed *Earlier today* section.
- The timer's alarm stops beeping after one minute. Its window stays open, and the browser tab keeps blinking.

## For people who build the app

- `./test.sh` now reports a test script that stops in the middle as a failure ("stopped early"), instead of quietly running fewer checks.
- New defaults in `app/defaults.go`: `DefaultAIEnabled` (off) and `DefaultAIMaxChars` (20,000).
- New API: `POST /api/ai/subtasks`, `/api/ai/rewrite`, `/api/ai/tags`, `/api/ai/quick`, `/api/ai/summarize`, `/api/ai/test`, `/api/ai/unload`, `GET /api/ai`, `POST /api/estimate`; `POST /api/import/entries` takes `preview`, `only` and `folder`; `prog` takes `{add: N}`. The README's API table lists them all.
