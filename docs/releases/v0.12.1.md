## What's new

- **Search finds entries by their id.** Type or paste an entry id (for example `lzf7ft`) into the search field, the command palette (`Ctrl+K`), the command line (`todo list`) or the `query` of `list_entries`, and the entry is found. Only the whole id counts, so a few letters of it do not give random hits.
- **Early warning of 45 minutes.** A reminder can now warn you 45 minutes before, in addition to 5, 10, 15, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours and 1 day.

## Fixes

- **`get_tree` shows all levels.** The MCP tool, `GET /api/tree` and `todo tree` stopped at three levels, so deep projects were cut off. They now show everything; a `depth` still limits it.
