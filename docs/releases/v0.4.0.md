## What's new

- **Links between entries.** Type `[[` in the notes and pick an entry: the preview shows its current name as a link that opens it. Each entry lists the entries that link to it under *Linked from*. Works in Markdown and BBCode notes.
- **Repeat after done.** A `+` at the end of a repeat rule (`repeat:3d+`, or *Count from the day it is done* in the custom Repeat field) counts the next date from the day you finish the entry, not from its old date.
- **Day plan in Today.** A bar adds up the estimates of the todos due today and the timed events, and compares them with the time left in your work day. It turns red when the plan does not fit. Set the work day under Settings → Work day.
- **Project icons.** Pick an icon for a project instead of its square. The icon's colour shows the status.
- **Quick add autocomplete.** Typing `#` or `@` lists your existing tags and people, so `#Work` does not become a second `#work`.
- **Large notes editor.** Click *Expand* or press Alt+Enter to edit long notes in a large window.
- **Copy the id** with one click in the detail panel.
- **README:** a short "At a glance" feature list, Quick add examples and new screenshots.
